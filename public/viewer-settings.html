If your text is short or unclear, this setting gives the voice model a hint. It also selects the word list for the profanity filter.

Opt out of TTS

Turn on a setting to stop TTS from reading your messages. You can turn off a setting that you turned on. If a moderator opted you out of a channel, only a moderator can undo that.

Opt out in every channel

TTS does not read your messages in any channel. TTS still announces your subs, cheers and gifts, but not the message that you add. If you redeem the TTS reward, TTS reads that message. This applies to Twitch only.

Opt out in this channel

TTS does not read your messages in this channel.
